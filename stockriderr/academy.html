What You'll Learn

Core Curriculum

Six pillars that form the foundation of every successful trader trained at Stock Rider Academy.

01
Market Fundamentals

Understanding markets, instruments, participants, sessions, and how global macro events move prices.

02
Technical Analysis

Advanced chart reading, price action, candlestick patterns, multi-timeframe analysis, and indicator confluence.

03
Risk Management

Position sizing, stop-loss strategies, risk-reward ratios, drawdown management, and capital preservation.

04
Smart Money Concepts

Institutional order flow, liquidity zones, market structure, order blocks, fair value gaps, and smart money tracking.

05
Trading Psychology

Emotional discipline, FOMO management, revenge trading prevention, journaling, and building consistent habits.

06
Live Trade Analysis

Real-time market analysis, live trade entries, post-trade reviews, and performance evaluation with mentor feedback.
