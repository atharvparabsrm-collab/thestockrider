Our History

The Academy Story

2020
Stock Rider Academy Founded

Shubham Chabuk Patil started Stock Rider Academy in Kolhapur in 2020 with a small batch of students and a strong vision for financial education and independence.

2021
Expanded to Online Mentorship

Launched online sessions and courses, allowing students from across Maharashtra and India to learn trading remotely.

2022
Crypto Program Introduced

Introduced cryptocurrency trading education covering Bitcoin, altcoins, and modern market strategies as demand increased.

2024
500+ Students Milestone

Trained over 500 students across different markets, building a strong foundation of practical trading knowledge.

2025
1000+ Students & Premium Community

Crossed 1000+ students and launched a premium trading community with regular insights and mentorship.

2026
Continuing to Grow

Stock Rider Academy continues to grow with more students, improved strategies, and a mission to spread financial awareness across India.
