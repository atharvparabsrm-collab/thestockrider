The Story

From Market Learner to Trading Master

Shubham Chabuk Patil is not your typical trading mentor. Born and raised in Kolhapur, Maharashtra, he built his expertise through 8+ years of disciplined market participation across multiple asset classes — Forex, Cryptocurrency, Indian Futures & Options, and Swing Trading.

His journey in markets began with a commitment to mastery through consistent learning, technical excellence, and emotional discipline. While also serving in the Indian Army as a GD soldier, Shubham refined his trading strategies and developed a unique mentorship approach — combining military discipline with practical market expertise.

In 2018, he founded Stock Rider Academy with a single mission: to help everyday Indians achieve financial independence through real, practical market education and disciplined trading execution.
